# Support

## Bugs and feature requests

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/nimiq/nimiq-pay-app/issues/new/choose) and pick the template that fits. Search existing issues first; if one already describes your problem, add your details there.

Include the app version, platform and OS version, and steps to reproduce. Never post your recovery phrase, PIN or private keys. Nobody from Nimiq will ask for them.

## Security issues

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Builds you compiled yourself

Builds from this repository are not official releases. See [Official releases and community builds](./README.md#official-releases-and-community-builds). We can help with problems in the source code, but not with your local signing, store accounts or service credentials.
