# Changelog

All notable changes to Nimiq Pay.

## 2.19.0 — 2026-07-23

### Mini Apps competition showcase
Discover the latest competition entries in a new Mini Apps showcase.

_How to use:_ Open Mini Apps and explore the competition showcase.

### Easier wallet recovery
Get helpful word suggestions while entering your recovery phrase, making wallet recovery faster and less error-prone.

_How to use:_ Choose wallet recovery and start entering your recovery words to see suggestions.

### Clearer pending payments
A new hint explains when an incoming NIM payment is still pending, so you know it is on its way.

### More useful notifications
Payment notifications now have clearer details and include transaction memos when available.

## 2.18.1 — 2026-07-15

### Better Mini App icons
Mini App icons now load only when needed and are saved for faster access later, improving privacy and performance.

### Cleaner in-app browsing
Fixed extra spacing that could appear while viewing content inside Nimiq Pay.

### Clearer wording
Updated messages throughout the app to use more consistent, platform-neutral language.

## 2.18.0 — 2026-07-09

### Enter amounts in crypto or fiat
You can now type any amount in either the crypto amount or its fiat value, whichever is easier for you. The app converts it for you automatically.

_How to use:_ When sending or requesting, tap the amount field and switch between crypto and your local currency.

### Secure pending NIM in one tap
Got NIM waiting to be secured? Just tap your pending NIM on the home screen to lock it in right away instead of waiting.

_How to use:_ On the Home screen, tap your pending NIM balance to secure it.

### What's New in the app
See exactly what changed with each update. A friendly summary now appears after you update, with older release notes available anytime.

### Smoother Mini Apps
Your most recently used Mini Apps now appear first, each app shows up only once, and opening them is faster and more reliable. Apps without an image now show a clean fallback icon.

_How to use:_ Find your recent Mini Apps in the row at the bottom of the Home screen.

### Reliability & polish
Faster PIN entry, more dependable incoming-payment notifications, a correct app icon on notifications, and "Withdraw All" now clears every last bit of NIM. Biometric login is also disabled when no PIN is set, for better security.

## 2.17.0 — 2026-07-02

### Fixed app stuck on PIN screen
Some users got stuck on the PIN screen after updating. The app now clears out the leftover data that caused this and opens normally.

### More reliable startup
If the network has trouble loading when you open the app, Nimiq Pay now still unlocks and lets you in, then catches up on syncing afterward instead of freezing.
