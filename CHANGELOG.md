# Changelog

All notable changes to Nimiq Pay.

## 2.23.0 — 2026-10-30

### Better support chat
When our support team closes a chat, you can still read their last reply, even after restarting the app. To ask something else, just type a new message and a new chat starts.

### Correct USDT balances
Some USDT amounts were shown 10 times higher than they really were. They now show the right value.

### Faster for busy wallets
Wallets with a long transaction history load and sync much faster. The home screen also stays smooth right after you unlock the app.

### Safer payments and funds
You're never charged a higher fee than the one you approved. Funds that got stuck or split after a reinstall or restore are now recovered automatically. We also fixed several issues with swaps, refunds and payment requests.

### Smoother everyday use
Unlocking with your PIN, scanning QR codes and moving between screens are more reliable. Mini Apps also get stronger security protections.

## 2.22.0 — 2026-10-06

### Safer swap fees and refunds
The fee you see before a swap is now the most you'll pay, even if network fees go up while you confirm. Refunds from failed swaps are also tracked more reliably, so your funds come back and show up correctly.

### More reliable syncing
The app recovers better when the network connection drops or a sync step fails, so your balance and transactions stay up to date during longer sessions.

### Clearer wallet status on startup
Your funded wallet no longer shows "Processing funds" for a while after you open the app. Claimed cashback now also updates in your transaction list right away.

### Smoother screens and Mini Apps
When the keyboard is open, the Withdraw button stays in view and the screen moves up properly, for example when you enter your PIN. Mini App logos now show in full in their dialogs. USDT payments now show a clear notice during scheduled maintenance.

### Crypto Map removed
The Crypto Map service has been shut down, so its button and map views are no longer in the app.

## 2.21.1 — 2026-09-28

### Share app logs with support
Support can now help you faster. You can send your app logs directly in the support chat, so the team can see what went wrong.

_How to use:_ Open the support chat, tap the attach button next to the message field, and confirm to send your logs. If a support agent asks for your logs, the chat will show a notice to remind you.

### Lightning payments in Mini Apps
Mini Apps can now request Bitcoin Lightning payments. The app also saves each payment attempt, so you are not charged twice.

### Better Mini Apps experience
Mini Apps can now use fullscreen for a more immersive view. With your permission, they can also see your NIM balance, so they can show you relevant options.

### Safer USDT payments
USDT payments and swaps now recover more reliably if something goes wrong along the way. The app will not retry a payment until it confirms the first one did not go through, so you are never charged twice.

### Chat button fixes
The support chat button and notification badge now stay in the right spot, even when a backup reminder is on screen.

## 2.20.0 — 2026-09-16

### Withdraw your way
Nimiq Pay now remembers whether you enter withdrawal amounts in crypto or your local currency.

_How to use:_ When withdrawing, choose crypto or your local currency for the amount. Your choice is saved for next time.

### More reliable payments
Payment results appear sooner, and pending transactions recover more reliably after restarting or reconnecting. Fixes also help prevent stuck payments and missing transaction history.

### Smoother Mini Apps
Mini Apps now support downloads. Navigation fixes keep controls accessible and prevent the back action from unexpectedly closing Mini Apps.

### Find competition entries faster
Search and filters make it easier to browse Mini Apps competition entries.

_How to use:_ Open the Mini Apps competition and use search or filters to narrow down the entries.

### Live chat for everyone
Live support chat is now available to all users, making it easier to get help when you need it.

## 2.19.1 — 2026-08-12

### Cashlinks you can just scan
Scanning a Cashlink now works end to end: the scanner closes on its own once the funds are claimed, and the claim shows up in your history and notifications with the sender's name instead of an unlabeled entry.

_How to use:_ Tap the scan button on the home screen and point your camera at a Cashlink code. Once it's claimed, the scanner closes automatically and you'll find the payment in your transaction list.

### Smoother stablecoin transfers
Fixed the connections used for stablecoin balances and fee estimates, so sending and receiving is more reliable and fees are calculated correctly.

### Ready for the network upgrade
Updated to the latest Nimiq network version so your wallet stays fully in sync and compatible after the network upgrade.

## 2.19.0 — 2026-07-23

### Mini Apps competition showcase
Discover the latest competition entries in a new Mini Apps showcase.

_How to use:_ Open Mini Apps and explore the competition showcase.

### Easier wallet recovery
Get helpful word suggestions while entering your recovery phrase, making wallet recovery faster and less error-prone.

_How to use:_ Choose wallet recovery and start entering your recovery words to see suggestions.

### Clearer pending payments
A new hint explains when an incoming NIM payment is still pending, so you know it is on its way.

### More useful notifications
Payment notifications now have clearer details and include transaction memos when available.

## 2.18.1 — 2026-07-15

### Better Mini App icons
Mini App icons now load only when needed and are saved for faster access later, improving privacy and performance.

### Cleaner in-app browsing
Fixed extra spacing that could appear while viewing content inside Nimiq Pay.

### Clearer wording
Updated messages throughout the app to use more consistent, platform-neutral language.

## 2.18.0 — 2026-07-09

### Enter amounts in crypto or fiat
You can now type any amount in either the crypto amount or its fiat value, whichever is easier for you. The app converts it for you automatically.

_How to use:_ When sending or requesting, tap the amount field and switch between crypto and your local currency.

### Secure pending NIM in one tap
Got NIM waiting to be secured? Just tap your pending NIM on the home screen to lock it in right away instead of waiting.

_How to use:_ On the Home screen, tap your pending NIM balance to secure it.

### What's New in the app
See exactly what changed with each update. A friendly summary now appears after you update, with older release notes available anytime.

### Smoother Mini Apps
Your most recently used Mini Apps now appear first, each app shows up only once, and opening them is faster and more reliable. Apps without an image now show a clean fallback icon.

_How to use:_ Find your recent Mini Apps in the row at the bottom of the Home screen.

### Reliability & polish
Faster PIN entry, more dependable incoming-payment notifications, a correct app icon on notifications, and "Withdraw All" now clears every last bit of NIM. Biometric login is also disabled when no PIN is set, for better security.

## 2.17.0 — 2026-07-02

### Fixed app stuck on PIN screen
Some users got stuck on the PIN screen after updating. The app now clears out the leftover data that caused this and opens normally.

### More reliable startup
If the network has trouble loading when you open the app, Nimiq Pay now still unlocks and lets you in, then catches up on syncing afterward instead of freezing.
